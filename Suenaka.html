The Tradition

About

Suenaka-kei Tetsugaku-hō Aikidō continues the study and practice of the aikidō transmitted by Roy Yukio Suenaka.

Rooted in Charleston, South Carolina, the tradition emphasizes practical martial effectiveness, disciplined training, and the philosophical principles of aikidō.

Its governing association, the Suenaka-kei Yūdanshakai, is being formed to bring together qualified practitioners and support the continued transmission of the art.

The Lineage Founder

Roy Yukio Suenaka

末中 幸男
末中 幸男
1940–2020
Honolulu, Hawaii
Charleston, South Carolina

Roy Yukio Suenaka was born in Honolulu, Hawaii. His martial education began under his father, Warren Kenji Suenaka, who supervised his early training and selected his instructors.

He began studying aikidō with Kōichi Tōhei in 1953 and subsequently trained under Morihei Ueshiba, the founder of aikidō, at the Aikikai Hombu in Japan beginning in 1961.

His martial education also included extensive study of jūdō and jūjutsu under Kazuo Itō.

Suenaka moved to Charleston, South Carolina, in 1972, establishing a lasting tradition of aikidō instruction in the American Southeast.

He authored Complete Aikido: Aikido Kyohan , published in 1998.

The Martial Art

Aikidō

合気道

The Aikidō of Roy Suenaka

Suenaka's aikidō combines the teachings he received from Morihei Ueshiba and Kōichi Tōhei with his extensive experience in practical martial arts.

Training emphasizes effective self-defense, balance, coordinated body movement, blending with attacks, throws, joint controls, and the development of mind and body.

Equal emphasis is placed on physical ability and personal development.

The practice encourages self-discipline, moderation, compassion, and respect for others.

Recognition & Certification

Rank & Transmission

段位と伝承

Suenaka-kei Tetsugaku-hō Aikidō distinguishes recognized aikidō rank from proficiency and transmission within its own martial tradition.

The intended system provides two complementary forms of recognition: Aikikai rank and Suenaka-kei certificates and licenses administered through its governing Yūdanshakai.

External Rank Recognition

Aikikai Kyū / Dan

合気会・級位／段位

The intended rank pathway follows the Aikikai system of kyū and dan grades.

Dan grades are examined, recognized, and registered with Aikikai Hombu through appropriately authorized Aikikai examination and registration channels.

Advancement remains subject to Aikikai requirements, qualifying practice, examination, and approval by authorized examiners.

Kyū (級) Student grades preceding black belt rank. Dan (段) Recognized black belt and higher grades.

The Yūdanshakai does not independently award Aikikai dan grades.

Internal Proficiency & Transmission

Suenaka-kei

末中系・目録／免許

Proficiency in Suenaka-kei Tetsugaku-hō Aikidō is to be evaluated and documented independently by the Suenaka-kei Yūdanshakai.

Its proposed certification system distinguishes the knowledge and technical proficiency a practitioner has demonstrated from the authority entrusted to teach.

Mokuroku (目録) Certificate or register of transmission.

Documents a defined body of techniques, principles, and proficiency received and demonstrated by the practitioner.

Menkyo (免許) License of teaching authorization.

Grants permission to teach the particular body of material specified in the license.

These credentials will be issued under standards established by the Yūdanshakai as its governance and examination procedures are formally adopted.

Levels of Transmission

Shoden (初伝) Initial Transmission Mokuroku / Menkyo
Chūden (中伝) Intermediate Transmission Mokuroku / Menkyo
Okuden (奥伝) Deep Transmission Mokuroku / Menkyo

Aikikai rank and Suenaka-kei transmission credentials are separate qualifications. Neither automatically grants the other. A teaching license does not, by itself, authorize the holder to issue further certificates or licenses. The Suenaka-kei certification framework is under development and does not represent historical licenses issued by Roy Suenaka.

Governance

The Yūdanshakai

末中系有段者会 Suenaka-kei Yūdanshakai Suenaka-Lineage Black Belt Association

The Suenaka-kei Yūdanshakai is being established as the governing association of Suenaka-kei Tetsugaku-hō Aikidō.

The association is intended to unite qualified dan-ranked practitioners connected through Roy Suenaka's teachings.

Its proposed responsibilities include maintaining the technical curriculum, evaluating Suenaka-kei proficiency, administering certificates of transmission and teaching licenses, and preserving the continuity of the tradition.

Aikikai rank recognition and registration will remain under the authority of appropriately authorized Aikikai organizations.

Founding membership, examination standards, and formal governing authority are currently being established.
