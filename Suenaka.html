The Association

About

The Suenaka-kei Yūdanshakai is an independent association in formation, bringing together black belt practitioners connected through the martial teachings of Roy Yukio Suenaka.

Based in Charleston, South Carolina, the association is dedicated to the continued study, practice, and transmission of his aikidō.

The Lineage

Roy Yukio Suenaka

末中 幸男
末中 幸男
1940–2020
Honolulu, Hawaii
Charleston, South Carolina

Roy Yukio Suenaka was born in Honolulu, Hawaii. His martial education began under his father, Warren Kenji Suenaka, who supervised his early training and selected his instructors.

He began studying aikidō with Kōichi Tōhei in 1953 and subsequently trained under Morihei Ueshiba, the founder of aikidō, at the Aikikai Hombu in Japan beginning in 1961.

His martial education also included extensive study of jūdō and jūjutsu under Kazuo Itō.

Suenaka moved to Charleston, South Carolina, in 1972, establishing a lasting tradition of aikidō instruction in the American Southeast.

He authored Complete Aikido: Aikido Kyohan , published in 1998.

The Martial Art

Aikidō

The Aikidō of Roy Suenaka

Suenaka's aikidō combines the teachings he received from Morihei Ueshiba and Kōichi Tōhei with his extensive experience in practical martial arts.

Training emphasizes effective self-defense, balance, coordinated body movement, blending with attacks, throws, joint controls, and the development of mind and body.

Equal emphasis is placed on physical ability and personal development.

The practice encourages self-discipline, moderation, compassion, and respect for others.
